See where you actually stand

Type nine figures off a Form 1040 in, or load the sample return, and get your tax position drawn out rather than listed: what the money did on its way to a tax bill, what the year worked out to, and where the planning questions sit. It is free, it runs entirely in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or stored.

Open the preview

It sits on the Tools page, beside the simulations of the individual rules it runs into. Load the sample return and see the shape of it before you put your own figures anywhere.
