Tax policy

TaxVisual focuses on how the rules apply to individual and business cases, not on policy analysis. For policy, these are the organisations we read:

  • Urban-Brookings Tax Policy Center — analysis and distributional estimates of federal tax policy
  • Tax Foundation — federal and state tax data, rates and maps
  • PolicyEngine — open-source tax and benefit microsimulation
  • Penn Wharton Budget Model — budget and economic effects of policy proposals
  • Congressional Budget Office — official budget and revenue projections
  • IRS Statistics of Income — the source data behind our dashboard
