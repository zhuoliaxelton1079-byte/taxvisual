The textbook

The open access book the tools draw on.

Fundamentals of Federal Taxation

An open access Pressbook covering individual, corporate, and pass-through taxation. It is free to read online, with no account and no paywall.

TaxVisual's tools are informed by the book but are not a strict chapter-by-chapter companion. They cover whatever topics are most useful to visualize, drawing loosely on its subject areas — so you can read the book for the rules and policy context, then use a tool to test what the rules actually do to a number.
