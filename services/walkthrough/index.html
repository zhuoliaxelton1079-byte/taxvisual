Return walkthroughs

A short recorded walkthrough of a finished return, from Form 1040 down to the documents behind it. Your client sees why the number is what it is — and you skip the follow-up emails.

What your client receives

  • A 5–10 minute video tracing the return from the bottom line back to the W-2s, 1099s, K-1s and schedules that produced it.
  • The figures that matter called out: effective rate, where the payments went, carryforwards and anything that will recur next year.
  • A one-page summary of the key figures, for the file.
  • All of it in your firm’s name.

Who it is for

  • CPA firms delivering a finished return to a client who wants to understand it.
  • Wealth advisors preparing for a review meeting with a client’s return in hand.
  • New-client onboarding, when last year’s return was prepared somewhere else.

What we need from you

  • The filed return as a PDF, through the secure portal we send you.
  • Anything the client has asked about, so the walkthrough answers it.
