We work alongside your preparer

TaxVisual does not prepare returns. We never compete for your client — we make the work you have already done easier to explain. Advisors whose clients need a preparer can ask us for a suggestion.

How an engagement works

  1. Send the case

    A short call to scope it. The return or the facts then come through a secure file portal — never by email.

  2. We build it

    Walkthrough, memo or visuals, with every figure traced to the return or to a cited rule. You review it before anything reaches your client.

  3. You deliver it

    Under your firm’s name: a link your client can open, and a PDF for the file.

Engagement terms

  • Pricing is quoted per engagement, after a short scoping call.
  • Confidentiality. Each engagement is covered by a written agreement. Client documents travel only through a secure file portal.
  • Responsibility. Analysis is delivered to you, the engaging professional. You remain responsible for the advice to your client.
