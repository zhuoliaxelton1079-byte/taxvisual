Planning packages

The decisions that matter this year, each worked through as an explorable memo your client can test.

What a package includes

  • A review of the prior-year return, to set the starting facts.
  • Two or three explorable memos on the decisions that matter, such as a sale, a conversion or a gift.
  • A projection of the current year under the options being considered.
  • Visuals ready for the client meeting, and a static PDF of every memo for the file.

Anatomy of an explorable memo

A conventional memo, with every number live.

Facts
The client’s figures as defaults — basis, sale price, holding period, income — each one adjustable.
Issue
One plain-language question the memo answers.
Law
The governing rules, cited to the Code and regulations, with a diagram of the order they apply in.
Analysis
Figures in the text recalculate as the facts change, with a chart beside each step.
Sensitivity
Which facts move the answer, and the thresholds where the conclusion flips.
Conclusion
The recommendation and the conditions that would change it — plus a static PDF for the file.

Typical questions

  • Where does a large capital gain land on top of this year’s income, and does the net investment income tax apply?
  • How much can be converted to a Roth before the next bracket or Medicare premium threshold?
  • Sell an appreciated asset now, give it away, or hold it for a step-up in basis?
  • How do owner compensation and the QBI deduction trade off for an S corporation?

How it is delivered

Each memo is a link that opens on exactly the scenario you discussed, in your firm’s name. Your client can adjust the facts and see the answer move; the static PDF records the version you advised on.

The first public sample memo — where a large capital gain lands on top of wage income — is in preparation. The model it is built on is already public: Comprehensive Tax Calculation.
