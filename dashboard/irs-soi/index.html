Read the data carefully. These are sample-based estimates of returns, not individual taxpayers or households. Overview shows return counts by filing status and AGI only. Joint / surviving spouse is paired with Married filing separately; Head of household is paired with Single. All four panels share the same AGI brackets and return-count scale. Filing-status splits are shown only where IRS sources publish them; other topics retain their source populations. The original brackets below $15,000 are combined into one band; higher AGI brackets remain as published. Amounts may not sum exactly because of rounding. “Unavailable” means the IRS withheld, combined, or did not report a value; it does not mean zero. A dagger (†) marks a small-sample estimate.
