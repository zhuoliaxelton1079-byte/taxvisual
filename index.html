Grounded in an open access tax textbook

TaxVisual's tools are informed by Fundamentals of Federal Taxation, an open access Pressbook covering individual, corporate, and pass-through taxation. Tools here aren't a strict chapter-by-chapter companion — they cover whatever topics are most useful to visualize, drawing loosely on the book's subject areas.

  • Read the open textbook for the underlying rules and policy context.
  • Use the tools here to test scenarios interactively, at your own pace.
  • New tools are added over time as the catalog grows.

Method and transparency

TaxVisual tools are educational simulations. They are not legal or tax advice. Each tool page documents its rate schedules, sources, update dates, and known limitations.
