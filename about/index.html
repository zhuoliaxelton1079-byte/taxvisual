About TaxVisual

TaxVisual publishes free, interactive tax policy tools for classrooms, policy workshops, and self-study. Each tool turns a piece of tax law — a bracket schedule, a basis calculation, a filing rule — into something you can manipulate directly in the browser rather than just read about.

The catalog grows over time; new tools are added as they're built, and it isn't tied to any single curriculum or textbook. It is loosely informed by topics covered in Fundamentals of Federal Taxation, an open access Pressbook, but tools here don't need to map to any particular chapter.

Questions, corrections, or tool suggestions: info@taxvisual.com.

TaxVisual publishes educational tax policy simulations. Not tax advice.
