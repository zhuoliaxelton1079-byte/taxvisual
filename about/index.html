About TaxVisual

A tax visualization practice for tax and wealth professionals.

Most tax conclusions reach clients as a table of numbers and a paragraph of caveats. TaxVisual exists because a picture of the mechanism — which dollars are taxed at which rate, which document feeds which line, where a threshold changes the answer — lands in a way the table never does.

How we work

  • Traceable. Every figure in a walkthrough or memo traces back to the return or to a cited rule.
  • Checked. Our public models are tested against independent figures — worked check figures, or the regulation’s own examples — and the tests run against the published page itself.
  • Behind you, not in front of you. We work for the professional who holds the client relationship, and deliver in their name.

What we don’t do

  • We do not prepare returns, so we never compete with the preparer.
  • We do not advise taxpayers directly. Analysis is delivered to the engaging professional, who remains responsible for the advice.
  • We do not do policy analysis. The resources page lists the organisations that do.
